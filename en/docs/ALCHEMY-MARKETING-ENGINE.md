## The Formula in One Line

**One idea → a full campaign** (landing pages, blogs, video scripts, email, PR, paid, social cuts) through a **graph** — a general step-by-step path the idea travels. At each step you can swap the harness, the loop, the tool, or the model to fit the campaign.

## Core Principles

1. **Everyone has the same models.** The edge is the graph, the brain, and the protocols — and you have to build those yourself.
2. **Model routing.** The plan, architecture and trade-offs run on a strong model (he uses opus 5). Narrow work underneath (resizing a thumbnail) gets cheap fast models.
3. **Eval gates at every step.** Humans and other agents evaluate and review output; weak work loops back.
4. **Two execution shapes:**
   - **Loop** — one task that has to clear a bar: the agent drafts, checks itself, fixes, keeps circling until it's good.
   - **Graph** — bigger many-part pieces: steps and routes drawn ahead of time, agents travel the map you laid down.
   - Usually a bit of both.

## The Path: Planning (1–5)

1. **The idea in** — dictate the whole thing out loud, every half-formed thought. The skill bundle catches the mess and hands back a starting brief.
   *(The Karpathy point: get it out of your head first, clean it up after.)*
2. **Ideation** — off that brief, open the idea into angles and directions to choose from. Throw most of them out.
3. **Research and context** — how much depends on the campaign:
   - Internal: the company brain (gBrain) — voice, past campaigns, what converted, the offers, the ICP.
   - External: market, competitors, the hooks working this week.
4. **Synthesis** — different models merge everything into a draft plan. The plan, architecture and trade-offs run on a strong model; the narrow work gets cheap fast models.
5. **The sign-off** — nothing crosses into execution until a human signs it off. Read the draft against marketing protocols, voice rules, brand, SOPs. Cut, sharpen, or send it back.

## The Path: Execution (6–8)

6. **Handing it to the build** — the idea splits into all the parts a campaign needs. One task that has to clear a bar → loop. Bigger many-part pieces → graph.
7. **The models doing it** — routing runs in execution too; don't pay opus prices to resize a thumbnail.
8. **What stays with people** — some coordination, some assets made by hand. Work that needs taste, a relationship, or a client in the room goes to the agency team.

## The Feedback Loop

Results come back in: what got bookmarked, what converted, what died → **updates the brain** (gBrain) for the next campaign.

## Application for the Island

- **The Brain** = the grimoire + agent memory (campaign history, Island voice, what converted).
- **The Protocols** = the production cycle (Backup → Plan → Report → Choose → Build → Test → Report → Call), brand rules.
- **The Graph** = the idea's route through planning → sign-off → execution → feedback.
- **Eval gates** = human + agent reviews at every step before moving on.
- **Routing** = strong models for strategy, cheap models for routine.
